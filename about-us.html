Our Mission

Committed to Athletes, Character & Community

East County Aquatics (ECA) is a year-round, non-profit water polo program designed to build athletes' skills while improving overall fitness. We are committed to developing not just better water polo players, but better teammates, students, and people.

ECA welcomes athletes ages 7–18+, from complete beginners who want to try a fun, team-oriented sport, to experienced competitors looking to sharpen their skills and compete at a high level.

Who We Are

A Program Run By the Community, For the Community

ECA was founded in 2009 by Clint & Marcy McLaughlin, and is classified as a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization by the standards of the Internal Revenue Service — donations are tax-deductible. Our success depends on the generosity and effort of our community — donations, sponsors, fundraisers, and, most of all, volunteers. Parents, coaches, and alumni all play a part in keeping the program strong and accessible for East County families.

Our program is proudly based in El Cajon, serving athletes throughout San Diego's East County.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

💪

Athletic Development

Age-appropriate coaching that builds swimming fitness, water polo IQ, and fundamental skills at every level.

🌟

Character First

Teamwork, discipline, and sportsmanship are as much a part of practice as shooting and passing drills.

🏡

Community Rooted

A family-run, volunteer-powered club that keeps water polo accessible for East County kids and teens.
