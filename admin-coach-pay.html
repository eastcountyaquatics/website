Coach Pay

← Back to Admin

Pay is calculated from practice and meeting hours (logged on the Attendance or Coaches’ Hours Logged page) plus hours on the Tournament Coach Hours page times each coach's hourly rate (set on the Coaches page), plus $75 per travel day for out-of-county tournaments. Recording a payment here just logs that it happened — it doesn't change what's shown as owed for a different date range.

Month-End Hours Sign-Off

After the end of each month, every coach with logged hours is emailed a link to review them and either sign off or request edits. Their response answer is emailed to Finance.SD.ECA@gmail.com and is reflected here. If changes to hours are needed, they are corrected and another email will be sent with the corrected hours.
