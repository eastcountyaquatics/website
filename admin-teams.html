Every team the club runs. The order here is the order teams appear in admin lists and on the site. Add a team and it gets its own page (edit its words in Edit Website), a spot in the Teams menu, and — once you set its ages — the team finder and registration offer it to the right athletes. Retire a team to take it off the site without losing its history.
