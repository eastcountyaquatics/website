Needs a Look

Payments that came in on Zeffy but couldn't be tied to an athlete automatically — usually a different email than the family's ECA account, or a misspelled name. Pick the athlete and click Assign; they're registered right away.

Payment Form for Site Prices

Tournaments set to Zeffy on Admin → Tournaments send families to one Zeffy form with the amount and a payment code from the site — prices and discount codes stay on this site. Build it once in Zeffy: an event form with a single pay-what-you-want ticket (minimum $1) and one required question on the ticket, “Payment code (from your ECA invite)”. Then click Use for Site Prices on it below.

Zeffy Forms

Tell the site what each Zeffy form is for. Registration and Masters forms register the athlete; Tournament forms mark that tournament paid. Set this before sharing a new form — a form's first payment shows up here as “Other” until you do. Each form should have a question with “Athlete” in it (per ticket on multi-athlete forms) asking for the athlete's full name.
