FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything families ask us before joining ECA.

What sessions are available and how long are they?

Our club cycle starts in the fall, with 4 sessions a year, each approximately 10–12 weeks:

  • 🍂
    Fall
    September–November (youth and high school girls only)
  • ❄️
    Winter
    November–February (youth and high school boys only)
  • 🌸
    Spring
    February–May
  • ☀️
    Summer
    May–July

Do we have to play every session?

No — ECA is happy to accommodate multi-sport athletes. That said, athletes who want to be part of the competitive teams are encouraged to participate year-round.

Can we try it for free before signing up?

Yes. ECA regularly offers free clinics for beginners, and new members can try practices free for 2 weeks. A USA Water Polo free trial membership is required to get in the pool (see below).

What age group is my athlete?

Age group is determined by the athlete's age on August 1 at the end of the cycle. For example, the age group for Fall 2026 depends on age as of August 1, 2027.

How do I register with USA Water Polo?

All ECA members must register with USA Water Polo (a 2-week trial registration is available for new athletes). Go to usawaterpolo.com and follow the online registration instructions. Use club #15690 and sign up as a Bronze member at minimum — athletes attending Junior Olympics need Gold-level membership. Memberships run January–December and must be renewed annually.

How do I order ECA gear?

Our store opens at the start of each session — ECA notifies families and posts a link on our Gear page.

Are there any discounts available?

ECA offers a sibling discount as well as scholarships for families in need (the prior year's tax return is required to validate a scholarship request). Contact us for more information.

Who can join?

Athletes ages 6–18 are welcome — no prior water polo experience necessary. There are minimum swimming requirements for each age group.

Do you teach kids to swim?

Due to limited pool availability, ECA can't provide swim instruction at this point. Coaches encourage athletes to join a local swim team to improve their swimming abilities.

What equipment is necessary?

A swimsuit and towel are enough to get started. The club has all necessary equipment for practices, but many members bring their own water polo ball to practice ball-handling on their own time. Members of our traveling competitive teams are asked to purchase a club suit and t-shirt — jammers and board shorts aren't allowed at certain tournaments.

Does ECA care what high school an athlete attends?

No — ECA is focused on athlete development regardless of school affiliation.

It's raining. Is there practice?

Yes, teams practice in the rain. Practices are only canceled for weather when there's lightning.
