Guidelines & Etiquette

Guidelines & Etiquette

ECA is dedicated to building athletes, character, and community. We ask coaches, parents, and athletes to help us by following our club guidelines and etiquette.

For Coaches & Admin

Our Promise to Athletes

  • 🤝
    Respect.
    We promise to respect all athletes regardless of experience, skill, and ability, and treat every athlete as an individual working toward their full potential.
  • ⏰
    Professionalism.
    Punctuality, preparation, and conduct are priorities. Practice plans are prepared in advance, and we share session, tournament, and event information as early as pool managers and tournament organizers allow.
  • 💛
    A positive experience.
    We won't yell to belittle or intimidate — coaches do have to raise their voice to instruct during practice and games, but we focus on effort and attitude, and point out mistakes constructively.
  • 📚
    Continual improvement.
    We use our ECA coaching manual for consistency across age groups, and keep improving through performance evaluation and coaching education.
  • 🏊
    Responsible growth.
    We're always searching for more pool time and coaches, balancing accepting more athletes against keeping practices and rosters manageable.
  • ⚖️
    Small, balanced rosters.
    We won't bring oversized rosters to events. Our goal is real playing time and experience, with rosters balanced by skill and experience.
  • 🛟
    Safety.
    We ensure equipment is safe and used properly, and never ask an athlete to participate while sick or injured.
  • 📜
    Follow the rules of the sport.
  • 🌊
    Our club motto.
    Building athletes, character, and community.
  • 😄
    Have fun!
    We coach because we love water polo, and love making this a positive, rewarding experience for athletes and families.
For Athletes

Athlete Guidelines

  • 📅
    Attend every practice and competition
    you can — time-management and a commitment to school studies matters, so be responsible to school and family commitments so you're available.
  • ⏰
    Arrive on time
    to every practice and competition — take responsibility at home to be ready so your parents can bring you on time.
  • ✉️
    Communicate absences in advance.
    If you'll miss a scheduled practice, tell your coach ahead of time — this is your activity, your team, and your coach.
  • 🙇
    Respect your coaches.
    Listen when they're speaking, give your best effort and attitude, and never leave early without telling your coach.
  • 🤜🤛
    Respect your teammates.
    Value every teammate regardless of skill or experience, and work hard for them. Bullying is not tolerated.
  • 🧑‍⚖️
    Respect officials.
    Never argue with an official or question their calls — play and adjust to the call.
  • 🤝
    Respect your opponents.
    Be a good sport — without opponents, there's no competition.
  • 🏅
    Respect the club.
    Represent ECA with sportsmanship and dignity. Wear your ECA suit and shirt at competitions, support other ECA teams when you can, and remember younger athletes look up to you as a role model.
  • 📏
    Play by the rules.
    Never cheat, bully, or intimidate an opponent to gain an advantage. Violence is not tolerated.
  • 😄
    Have fun!
    Practice and compete because you love water polo and love being part of a team.
For Parents

Parent Guidelines

  • 📖
    Review the Athlete Guidelines with your athlete.
    Coaches present these at the start of every session, but we appreciate parental support reinforcing them.
  • 💳
    Pay session and tournament fees promptly.
    ECA offers scholarships to families in need — contact Coach Clint at eastcountyaquatics@gmail.com with a request (last year's tax return is required to validate it).
  • ✅
    Reply promptly to club emails,
    especially tournament RSVP forms — we face real deadlines entering teams in tournaments, and a roster spot isn't guaranteed without a completed RSVP.
  • 🤫
    Let coaches coach.
    Refrain from coaching your child during practices and games, and please don't approach the bench or team during competition.
  • 🚫
    Never approach or speak/yell at an opposing coach, official, or referee.
    Let the coach handle situations during or after the game.
  • 🎯
    Focus on effort and attitude,
    rather than mistakes or winning/losing.
  • ⚖️
    Understand rosters are kept small and balanced
    where possible — late cancellations after an RSVP put the team in a difficult spot, and a roster spot isn't guaranteed just by RSVPing. We're still a growing club and appreciate your patience with this goal.
  • 💬
    Raise concerns the right way.
    Please don't approach a coach right after a game or during practice to question tactics or playing time, and please don't discuss it with other parents. Email Coach Clint and/or Coach Scott — we reply to every email — or use our contact form.
  • 🗣️
    Encourage your child to talk to their coach directly
    when appropriate.
  • 🙋
    Volunteer when you can.
    ECA relies on parent support for fundraising, transporting equipment, running desks at hosted tournaments, and team social events. We greatly appreciate it!
  • 😄
    Have fun!
    Remember, your child plays water polo for their enjoyment — and you should too.
