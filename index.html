A club built on water polo & community

East County Aquatics gives East County San Diego athletes a place to learn the game, get stronger, and compete — while making lifelong friends along the way. Whether your athlete is picking up a water polo ball for the first time or chasing a spot on a Junior Olympics roster, there's a team for them at ECA.

See programs & age groups

  • Skill-first coaching.Age-appropriate practices focused on fundamentals, fitness, and love of the game.
  • Season-long commitment.Practices and games run year-round across multiple East County pool locations.
  • Stay connected.Teams use the BAND app and group chats for weekly updates. Once your athlete is registered, their team’s BAND link appears in your account.
  • Non-profit & affordable.Supported by donations, sponsors, and fundraisers to keep the sport accessible.

New to ECA? Try 2 weeks first

We go all year long — spring, summer, fall, and winter. Athletes new to ECA are welcome to come try us out! For practice times and locations, see the Teams page and select your athlete's age group. This is a “try before you buy” opportunity. If your athlete would like to continue after the trial, we’ll prorate the session back to the beginning of the trial period. If it’s not for your athlete, you owe nothing! Does not apply to Splashball or Spring/Summer League.

Questions? Email eastcountyaquatics@gmail.com
