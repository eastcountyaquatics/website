Junior Olympics

Junior Olympics Water Polo

ECA's competitive age-group teams train and compete with an eye toward Junior Olympics qualification each summer.

The Pathway

Competing on the Biggest Stage in Age-Group Water Polo

USA Water Polo's Junior Olympics (JO) tournament is the premier summer event for age-group club water polo, bringing together teams from across the country. ECA's competitive teams train throughout the season to prepare for JO qualifying tournaments and championship play.

Age divisions typically include 10U, 12U, 14U, 16U, and 18U for both boys and girls, competing in regional qualifiers ahead of the summer championship events.

Want to Compete?

Athletes on our competitive age-group teams (12U, 14U, and High School) are the primary pathway to JO competition. Reach out to learn which team fits your athlete.
