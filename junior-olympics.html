Junior Olympics

Junior Olympics Water Polo

ECA's competitive age-group teams train and compete with an eye toward Junior Olympics qualification each summer.

The Pathway

The Largest Age-Group Tournament in the World

USA Water Polo Junior Olympics is the largest age-group water polo tournament in the world. Athletes and teams of all skill and experience levels come from across the country to compete on the national stage — the pinnacle club water polo event that marks the end of the season. Teams compete over a 4-day period within their age group, alternating between even years in San Jose and odd years in Orange County.

Qualifying

How Teams Qualify

Teams must qualify for one of the three divisions of Junior Olympics (Championship, Classic, and Invitational). Each USA Water Polo zone hosts a qualifying tournament — Regional JOs — in May/June to determine which teams advance. 10U/12U/14U Regional qualifiers are held throughout San Diego, typically around May 28 – June 4. Athletes considered for a roster spot must be available for all days of both the qualifying tournament and Junior Olympics — please talk to coaches before booking summer family vacations.
