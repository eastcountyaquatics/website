Masters Water Polo

Adult water polo for East County players who want to stay active, competitive, and connected to the game.

Adult Program

Water Polo Doesn't Stop at Graduation

ECA's Masters program gives adult athletes — former high school and club players, or anyone new to the game — a way to keep playing water polo in a fun, social, and competitive environment.

All fitness levels and experience levels are welcome. Come for the exercise, stay for the community.

Ask About Masters
  • 🤽
    Open to All Levels
    Former players and newcomers to the sport are both welcome.
  • 🏊‍♂️
    Great Fitness
    A full-body, low-impact workout that builds strength and conditioning.
  • 🎉
    Social & Competitive
    Play for fun, fitness, or competition — it's up to you.
