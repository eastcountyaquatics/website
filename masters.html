Men's & Women's Masters

Welcome to Our Masters Water Polo Team!

Join our inclusive Masters Water Polo Team for players aged 18 and above, regardless of skill level. Whether you're a novice or a seasoned player, we're excited to have you on board.

Practices run twice a week — days, times, and pool locations can shift week to week depending on availability. Join our WhatsApp to stay current on the schedule.

Code of Conduct

At the heart of our team is a commitment to respect, camaraderie, and sportsmanship. Our primary objective is to have fun while staying fit. Any use of vulgar language, violence, or disrespect towards coaches, players, or referees will not be tolerated and may result in immediate removal from the team.

More Than a Team

Beyond the pool, we're a close-knit community that enjoys socializing and bonding beyond the game. Dive in with us and experience the joy of water polo, whether you're looking to play, compete, or make lasting friendships.

Stay Connected

We use group messaging apps to communicate weekly practice times and updates — ask us for the current WhatsApp link when you join.
