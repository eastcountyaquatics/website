Programs

A Program for Every Age & Skill Level

ECA runs year-round, offering pathways for ages 7–18+ — from first splashes to advanced competitive water polo.

Pathway

From First Splash to Competitive Play

Every athlete starts somewhere. ECA's programs are structured so beginners build confidence and fundamentals, while experienced players are challenged to grow and compete.

🌊

Introductory / Beginner

For athletes new to water polo. Focused on water comfort, basic swimming fitness, ball handling, and the fundamentals of the game in a fun, low-pressure environment.

🤽‍♂️

Youth & Middle School

Age-group teams (including 12U and 14U) build on fundamentals with structured practices and introduce competitive tournament play.

🏅

High School & Competitive

Advanced training for high school-age athletes, with an eye toward high-level competition and Junior Olympics qualification.

Good to Know

Practices & Communication

Practice days, times, and pool locations vary by age group and can change throughout the season. Each team stays connected and up to date through its group chat / BAND app.

Exact practice schedules are managed per team. Email us and we'll get you connected with your athlete's team group.
  • 📍
    East County Pools
    Practices are held across multiple El Cajon-area and East County pool facilities.
  • 📱
    Team Group Chats
    Weekly schedules and updates are shared with each team via BAND / group text.
