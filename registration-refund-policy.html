Policy

Registration/Refund Policy

Thank you for participating in San Diego East County Aquatics. Registration fees cover equipment, pool rental, coaching fees, and other club expenses.

Registration Fees

Registration fees are required to participate and are non-refundable once the session has commenced.

Medical Withdrawals

Credit may be considered on a prorated basis, net administrative fees, if a participant withdraws for medical reasons supported by a doctor's note. Withdrawal requests must be submitted in writing to eastcountyaquatics@gmail.com at least 7 days before the program starts, including the reason for the request and supporting documentation if required.

Tournament Athlete Withdrawals

The tournament registration fee is non-refundable once the roster has been finalized and posted on the website. If a registered athlete withdraws due to medical reasons (supported by a doctor's note) or an unforeseen circumstance, a full or partial credit may be issued at ECA's discretion. Withdrawal requests must be submitted in writing to eastcountyaquatics@gmail.com at least 7 days before the tournament date to be considered.

Exceptions & Event Cancellations

Exceptions to this policy may be granted at the discretion of the ECA Director and Financial Director in exceptional circumstances. If a scheduled event (tournament, match, practice) is canceled for reasons beyond our control — weather, facility closure, acts of God — no refunds will be issued for that event.

Refund Requests

All refund requests must be submitted in writing to eastcountyaquatics@gmail.com, including the reason for the request. Requests are reviewed and processed within 7 business days.

Policy Changes

Participants will be notified of any changes to this policy in advance. By participating in San Diego East County Aquatics, you acknowledge and agree to abide by its terms.
