10U Coed — Ages 9–10

Fall season team info: schedule, coaches, and tournaments.

Practice

Practice Schedule

Athletes should be able to swim 50 yards freestyle without stopping and tread water for 2 minutes. Swim test on the athlete’s first day of practice. Additional fee — USA Water Polo Bronze level membership minimum required to play.
