Splashball — Ages 5–8

ECA's entry point into water polo — water safety skills and the basics of the game, at Grossmont High School Pool.

What Is Splashball?

A Fun Introduction to the Game

Splashball is an entry point into the sport of water polo. This program is a way for kids (ages 5–8) to enhance their water safety skills while being introduced to the basics of water polo. Participants learn the basic rules of the game, along with introductory movements and skills that help them become more confident in the water.

No experience necessary — it's first come, first served, with limited space in each session.

Splashball runs in seasonal sessions at Grossmont High School Pool. Athletes 7 and up can join our age-group teams this session — see the Teams page.
